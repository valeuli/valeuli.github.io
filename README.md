I care deeply about the quality of what I build. Before delivering a feature, I try to understand the real requirement, identify edge cases, write automated tests, test the complete flow manually, and monitor its behavior in production.

For me, software is not only about making something work. It should solve a real problem, reduce friction, and deliver value to the people using it.

## How I work

I enjoy collaborating with Product, Design, Data, Customer Success, and Engineering teams. I ask questions when requirements are unclear, consider whether customer-specific requests can become reusable product capabilities, and balance product value with reliability, maintainability, and delivery time.

Some principles that guide my work:

- Understand the problem before choosing the solution.
- Treat testing as part of development.
- Consider edge cases and failure scenarios from the beginning.
- Communicate risks and trade-offs clearly.

## My journey into engineering

My path into software engineering was not completely traditional. I began studying Systems Engineering in Venezuela. After moving to Colombia, I started my degree again while building my professional career. I first worked as a Backend Developer at Panda Group and later joined Platzi through a Customer Success role while continuing to prepare myself for the opportunity to move into engineering.

Working in Customer Success was an important part of my professional development. It allowed me to see a technology product from outside engineering and understand what happens when software problems reach users and the teams responsible for supporting them.

That experience still influences how I build software today. I think about the people using the product, the teams supporting it, the value we want to deliver, and the metric or behavior we expect to improve with each technical decision.
